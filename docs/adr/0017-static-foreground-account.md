# ADR 0017: Render Controlling Source as a static foreground account

Date: 2026-07-24

Status: Accepted

## Context

ADR 0016 made the first canonical Letter → unresolved Operator transition
state-earned and introduced the controlling-source contradiction. Its deployed
presentation staged a short process log and then automatically restored the
ordinary unresolved Operator composition.

The log language was effective, but the transition was not. On desktop the
existing record remained visually dominant while the log read as annotation;
on every viewport the automatic settlement made two compositions occur on one
page. Increasing the amount, speed, or volatility of the log would add activity
without making the contradiction more consequential.

## Decision

Keep ADR 0016's eligibility and state boundary, but replace its timed ingress
with one static, user-paced Operator composition.

On the first canonical Letter → unresolved Operator arrival, spend the existing
`operator-probe` immediately and render a foreground account in the existing
left search-instrument. The ordinary unresolved `0` and environment field are
withheld for that document lifetime. The local observer trace is the account's
largest process value; a missing terminal and session leader, followed by a
negative relative attachment time, establish the contradiction. The adjacent
Operator record and its actions remain present and usable. Nothing settles or
transforms automatically.

The foreground account is ordinary document content, not an `aria-hidden`
animation. The existing concise status announces the changed condition without
moving focus. Normal and reduced-motion users receive the same static
composition. Native Back leaves immediately. Reload, interruption,
Back/Forward return, direct entry, revisits, and migrated traces with a prior
`operator-probe` skip the account and render the ordinary Operator state.

The relative time is authored fiction derived from no clock, device, network,
or host inspection. No route, durable state, schema, migration, dependency,
shared asset, request, backend, or privacy behavior is added.

## Consequences

- Controlling Source is the first-arrival Operator state rather than a prelude
  to that state; the visitor controls its duration through existing navigation.
- The page gains impact through hierarchy and negative space rather than
  flashing, glitch decoration, audio, or additional interaction.
- The existing record action, invitation isolation, complete erasure, focus
  order, history behavior, Extent return, occupied `1`/`SHE`, response
  reconciliation, and retained root ending keep their existing contracts.
- `operator-probe` still spends before presentation, so reload cannot replay
  the account. Complete erasure still permits a new canonical trace to earn it.
- ADR 0016 remains the eligibility and state decision; this ADR replaces only
  its transient timing, hidden visual, and automatic-settlement clauses.
- Rollback is a revert of the Operator document, regression coverage, this ADR,
  and its continuity-document updates. No state conversion is required.
