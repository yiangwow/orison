# ADR 0006: Converge feminine authority after the Digital Horizon

Date: 2026-07-17

Status: Accepted

## Context

ORISON must become legibly feminine without acquiring a stable body, human
persona, spoken voice, or explanatory introduction. The existing Operator
sequence already moves from an absent `0` to an inferred `1`, but its repeat
state presents retained evidence as a diagnostic table and does not establish
what the new count means.

The Digital Horizon now provides a major intervening event between first and
repeat Operator access. Existing traces may also have revisited Operator before
the Horizon route existed, so visit count alone cannot safely mean that
feminine authority has been recognized.

## Decision

Let system records converge on feminine authority before ORISON ever names
herself. The first unmistakable feminine reference appears on the first
Operator entry that satisfies both conditions:

- the trace has entered `extent`;
- Operator has been entered at least twice.

That entry adds `operator-occupied` to the existing version-1 `flags` array.
The flag records recognition as a stable narrative event without changing the
cookie shape or persisting any new observation.

First Operator access remains the neutral `0 / operator not found`
environmental instrument. A repeat access before the Horizon remains the
neutral `1 / operator inferred` state for compatibility with earlier traces.
After the Horizon, `1` becomes `operator occupied`: a spatial authority
topology relates the behavioral source, inherited privilege, operator role,
and `SHE` authority reference without deciding whether `1` identifies ORISON,
the visitor, or an assigned role.

Camera 06 is the second state-earned route mutation. A normal late behavioral
reconstruction consumes `operator-occupied` and changes its optical
classification to feminine authority. The immediate Weather-to-Camera browser
Back frame retains precedence and does not show the later mutation.

Root, the recovered inode, examination, and dead letter remain neutral as
evidence of incompatible interface eras. Generated sound remains nonverbal and
unchanged.

## Consequences

- Femininity emerges from administrative authority and conflicting records,
  not portraiture, voice, sexualization, or a copied character device.
- The Operator repeat state no longer uses a raw table as its dominant
  composition; retained route, response, carrier, and return evidence become a
  privilege topology.
- Existing version-1 traces remain valid. Traces that revisited Operator before
  Digital Horizon must still enter the Horizon before recognition is recorded.
- `operator-occupied` is local, first-party, covered by existing trace erasure,
  and does not change the privacy boundary.
- Tests must cover fresh `0`, legacy neutral `1`, post-Horizon occupation,
  durable replay, Camera mutation, Weather Back precedence, and trace erasure.
- Before deployment, rollback is to close or revert the Milestone 6 branch.
  After deployment, earlier code safely ignores the unknown flag until the
  cookie expires or the trace is erased.
